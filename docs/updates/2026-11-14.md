# Family Alignment — 2026-11-14

## Scope

No literature sweep: the last full check remains 2026-09-28. Sections 1–5 of the README and `primary_semantics` in `registry/papers.yaml` now follow the five semantic families of the survey (Outcome, Absolute Progress, Relative, Temporal Value, Hybrid), which assigns a method by the question its native output answers. Where a record and Table S1 of the paper disagreed, the record was changed.

## Other changes

- `docs/update-protocol.md` defines the five families as the survey does.
- `docs/reward-model-benchmarks.md` and Section 9 of the README describe RRM-Bench-1K, the benchmark of the survey, in place of the earlier requirements blueprint.
- RBM-EVAL-OOD reads 571 trajectories on five robot embodiments, as in Table VII of the Robometer paper and the authors' note in [robometer/robometer#22](https://github.com/robometer/robometer/issues/22#issuecomment-4413819302); the earlier 976 came from the main text of the paper. The six public evaluation sets hold 24, 150, 27, 36, 304, and 30 entries (checked 2026-11-14).
- The project page shows the 114 methods of the paper's corpus (MetaWAM added under Temporal Value).

## Unresolved items

- The registry does not yet hold every method of the survey's corpus; the project page lists all of them by family and design route.
- RRM-Bench-1K has no record in `registry/benchmarks.yaml` yet; its data release is pending.
